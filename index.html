Dataset: Nashik locationsApproximate demonstration route distances and waste values. Not official municipal measurements.

Data Sources & Limitations

REAL LOCATION DATA: Nashik place names and civic context are referenced from public NMC and map/location sources.

APPROXIMATE PROJECT DATA: Coordinates, road names, distances, and estimated travel times are simplified academic route data.

DEMONSTRATION DATA: Waste levels and priorities are demonstration values, not live NMC measurements.

Solid-waste context: NMC Solid Waste Management Department and MoHUA Swachh Bharat Mission.

This prototype uses Graph + Min Heap + Dijkstra. It is not a live municipal dispatch system and does not require a paid Google Maps API.
